Suggested maintenance workflow

1. Review the observation
Confirm the record and inspect the area safely.
2. Prioritise and assign
Use qualified personnel for safety-critical or uncertain issues.
3. Record the outcome
Update the issue status and preserve notes/photos.
Visible photos cannot confirm structural safety. Seek a qualified professional for significant cracks, movement, exposed wiring or other potentially dangerous conditions.

General next steps

Document what you see
Record when it appeared and whether it changes.
Consider the source
For moisture, the source may need attention before cosmetic repairs.
Get appropriate help
Use a qualified tradesperson or professional when needed. Do not attempt unsafe electrical or structural repairs yourself.
BuildScan does not provide a professional diagnosis, verify contractors, or fetch live repair quotes in this offline prototype.

Issue report

Open a clean report page with your current issue records. In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF” to create a PDF.

Report includes issue titles, types, locations, priorities, statuses, assigned teams and descriptions.

Backup your records

Export your saved records to a JSON file so you can keep a copy or restore them in another browser.

Data and privacy

Records are stored locally in your current browser.
Clearing browser data may erase records. Download a backup regularly.
Records are not shared with a team or sent to a server.
Do not upload sensitive personal details or confidential site information to a public demo.

Prototype limitations

Image analysis, AI diagnosis, contractor verification, live quotes, predictive maintenance and cloud syncing are not implemented. The prototype records information you enter and helps organise it for review.
